Awards & Applications

Supporting innovative healthcare improvement projects and research initiatives that align with the NPLB mission of accountability, transparency, and equity in care delivery.

Funding Priorities & Evaluation Criteria

Applications are assessed against five priority areas. Projects should clearly address at least one of the following domains.

1

Healthcare System Accountability

Projects that institutionalise transparency, quality measurement, and governance frameworks within healthcare organisations.

2

Resource Optimisation

Innovations that improve allocation and utilisation of healthcare resources — including workforce, equipment, and capacity.

3

Patient Experience Enhancement

Solutions that directly improve patient outcomes, satisfaction, and equity of access across diverse populations.

4

Scalable Improvement Strategies

Initiatives with demonstrated potential for replication and adaptation across multiple healthcare settings and systems.

5

Data-Driven Decision Making

Projects that leverage analytics, predictive modelling, or real-time monitoring to drive measurable improvements in care delivery.
