Funding Priorities & Evaluation Criteria

  • Healthcare System Accountability - Projects that institutionalize transparency and quality improvement measures
  • Resource Optimization - Innovations that improve healthcare resource allocation and utilization
  • Patient Experience Enhancement - Solutions that directly improve patient outcomes and satisfaction
  • Scalable Improvement Strategies - Initiatives with potential for replication across healthcare systems
  • Data-Driven Decision Making - Projects using analytics to improve healthcare delivery
