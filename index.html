Canada has the highest emergency department use among peer countries, largely because of the inability of patients to access appropriate care elsewhere in the system. Grant Innes' work addresses causes and solutions for the growing crisis in emergency care. He believes that operational improvement can substantially reduce care delays throughout the system, and that the biggest barrier to process change is a lack of patient care accountability frameworks that define program accountability zones and unclear care expectations.

Dr. Innes has led successful change through the introduction of accountability strategies and operational improvement in British Columbia and Alberta. His recent diagnosis of pancreatic cancer and the hope his work could continue motivated him to launch the No Patient Left Behind health system improvement fund. The fund's mission is to promote and support operational improvement, to implement system accountability frameworks and strategies through policy advocacy, and to develop effective leaders who will drive system change that increases patient access to appropriate care in emergency departments, hospitals or communities.
