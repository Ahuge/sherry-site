Our Core Purpose

The mission, vision, and terms of reference that guide every initiative we undertake.

🎯

Mission

To create a more accountable, transparent, and effective healthcare system by equipping leaders with proven improvement strategies and fostering a culture of shared learning.

🔭

Vision

A world where every patient receives timely, high-quality care — and systemic failures are systematically identified, studied, and eliminated.

📋

Terms of Reference

We operate as a non-partisan collective of healthcare professionals, researchers, and policymakers committed to open-source dissemination of accountability frameworks and capacity-building tools.

The Grant Innes Tribute

A legacy of vision, compassion, and relentless pursuit of better care for every patient.

Honouring a Visionary

Grant Innes dedicated his career to proving that healthcare systems can — and must — be held to a higher standard. His work laid the foundation for the accountability frameworks, capacity strategies, and improvement resources this site exists to preserve and scale.

His vision was simple but profound: no patient should ever be left behind because of a failure in the system designed to help them.
