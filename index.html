Message from Dr Innes

During 40 years as an emergency physician, I dealt with many waiting room disasters and met many families who lost their loved one because of emergency department care delays. These people are the inspiration for No Patient Left Behind. This story began 25 years ago when I was working in a Vancouver emergency waiting room. Every chair was full. Someone was on the floor vomiting in a wastebasket. People were moaning. Two cops were restraining a psychotic patient in the corner. Six or seven paramedic crews were lined up in the hallway, unable to offload their patients, which meant they couldn't respond to emergencies in the community. It was chaos. No one could move to a care space because all of the stretchers were blocked by admitted patients waiting to be moved upstairs to hospital beds. The next week I applied to be department head.

Our Mission

Our mission is to fund health system improvements that reduce wait times for care in emergency departments, hospitals or communities, and to support strategies that increase efficiency or incorporate clear expectations for patient care (accountability).
